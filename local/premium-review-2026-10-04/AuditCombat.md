## Summary

1. **The advertised 2×/8× targets are floors, not effective difficulty.** With Combat hard, ordinary creatures average 2.20–6.44× raw HP and 1.85–2.80× outgoing damage before Karma/affixes; bosses average 8.40–16.88× raw HP and 2.27–2.75× damage. Player damage is also ×0.85. At Karma +3, even Meadows bosses average 20.40× HP. An eight-player target is plausible only as an empirical tuning target, not established by `Boss.health: 8`.
2. **BlacksmithingExpanded's main settings are synced but not locked.** Its separate SkillManager is locked; its main ConfigSync has neither `IsLocked = true` nor a locking entry. This is an active Pack-rule break, not fixable by adding an invented `Lock Configuration` key.
3. **Boss affixes produce composition lotteries.** Adaptive/vortex/chameleon can punish a class-specialized group much more than their nominal HP; `Boss Modifiers = Max1` is the simplest exact cap proposal. Keep ordinary affix rarity rather than returning to stock chances.
4. **Late-biome pressure is compounded primarily by stars + Karma, not a hidden exponential stat formula.** Recommend trial `Global.healthPerLevel: 0.75`, `Global.damagePerLevel: 0.15`, `Boss.damagePerLevel: 0.05`, and Karma thresholds `[90, 180]`; preserve fixed 2/8 health floors and hard world difficulty.
5. **Player power genuinely multiplies across owners.** EpicLoot weapon modification, Oathbound `SEMan.ModifyAttack`/target bonuses, and Herbalist ×1.25 are sequential; the smith contributes another up-to-10% physical weapon increase. AdditiveDamageModifier does NOT flatten these offensive multipliers. Keep the already modest smith/elixir tuning; tune enemy tails before cutting class identity.
6. **SeaAnimals receives biome scaling and hostile-kill class XP, but not automatic EpicLoot biome loot.** HP and stars are not the gap; named loot-table integration is. TheFisher is fish/items/pickables/aquariums, not a second hostile creature roster; Dive_In changes existing creature pursuit, it does not add monsters.
7. **Siege commanders are bespoke, not normal biome elites.** Oathbound resets commander HP to `180 + 180*tier`, caps their hits, and replaces ordinary drops. Generic CreatureManager start-time scaling can overlap, but Oathbound reasserts commander HP. Treat this as an explicit exception and measure it, not as verified parity.
8. **Companions/tames must count in boss power.** Hunter/Dragonsworn damage and health have their own level/talent formulas and runtime overrides; generic tames can retain high-star/modifier power. No confirmed exposed companion balance slider exists. Retain class pets, but measure boss nights with actual pets/tames, not eight players alone.

## 1. Effective multiplier chain and per-biome table

**Formula, relative to an unstarred normal-world vanilla prefab:** let rolled level be `L`, regional Karma bonus `K` (0–3 currently), and `N=L+K`.

- Ordinary raw HP: `2 * (1 + (N-1)*1)` = `2N`.
- Ordinary attack damage before armor/resistance: `1.5 * 1.2 * (1 + (N-1)*0.25)`.
- Regular boss raw HP: `8 * (1 + (N-1)*0.5)`.
- Boss attack damage: `1.5 * 1.5 * (1 + (N-1)*0.1)`.
- Player damage against nonplayers is ×0.85 under hard; effective HP against player DPS is therefore raw HP / 0.85. **Do not multiply HP by 1.5:** hard's 150 is enemy damage, not HP.
- Affixes then alter outgoing damage/received damage/uptime; these cannot honestly be represented as one scalar. Regional Karma adds levels, not a separate multiplicative percentage. Enforcers add their own +2 on top of regional bonus and use non-boss rules.
- No distance stat scaling is active in the enforced levels table; those example lines are commented out. Headcount HP/damage percentages are both zero and cap is one.

Evidence: `config/enforced/CreatureManager/levels.yml:50-58,97-103`; CreatureManager 1.2.5 decompile `:19177-19253,19599-19606` explicitly removes vanilla level HP growth when installing its own rule, so do not double-count vanilla stars; preset `:6840-6850`. Vanilla hard asset keys: `/tmp/lembitu-grill/CreatureManagerCombat/asset-evidence.json:34-42` (playerdamage 85, enemydamage 150, speed/size 110, level-up rate 120); vanilla damage consumers `Character.cs:2268-2272,2414-2418`. CreatureManager's own roll uses its weights (`:17969-17983`), not `Game.m_enemyLevelUpRate`; hard's vanilla level-up-rate key is not another ×1.2 applied to its preset weights. Vanilla speed/size is additional danger, not a HP multiplier.

**Important boss Karma nuance:** blocking Karma *gain while a boss is alive* does not prevent existing Karma from affecting its initial roll. `CreatureManager.cs:13710-13727` excludes players, not bosses; `:17969-17983` adds resolved Karma to any rolled eligible creature. Thus the K3 boss columns are real formula possibilities, not merely ordinary-creature pressure.

**Targets / month pace [INFERENCE]:** With eight equal vanilla-equivalent DPS players, a K0 Fader at 16.88× HP takes `16.88/(8*.85)=2.48` normal solo fight-times before their class/gear power. Two such players facing a normal Ashlands mob see `6.44/(2*.85)=3.79` solo-equivalent fight-times. If players gain 2× sustained DPS from the pack, these become 1.24 and 1.89 respectively. This demonstrates why the targets are plausible but unproven and why the ordinary late-biome tail is more worrying than the boss floor. At rolled level 6 + K3, ordinary HP is 18× and damage 5.4× before affixes; a boss is 40× HP/4.05× damage. A one-month, 50–60-hour Run should not depend on farming perfect gear to withstand those tails.

**Proposal (candidate tune, not empirical optimum):** retain `Combat hard`, `Global.health: 2`, `Global.damage: 1.2`, `Boss.health: 8`, `Boss.damage: 1.5`; change `Global.healthPerLevel: 0.75`, `Global.damagePerLevel: 0.15`, `Boss.damagePerLevel: 0.05`; retain `Boss.healthPerLevel: 0.5` until eight-person fight timing is measured. Change `karma.thresholds: [90, 180]` (cap +2, still reaches cap at 180) rather than turning Karma off. This removes the extra +3 tier and reduces ordinary damage tails while preserving specialization and group progression. All are server-owned keys. Confidence: high formula / medium-low proposed balance; Shakedown required for values.

## 2. Pack-rule break: smith settings sync without lock

`Smith.cs:4493-4499` creates the main `configSync` with version/mod-required fields only. `:4665-4672` syncs settings. Entire `SetupConfigs` (`:4872-4934`) binds no locking entry; startup (`:4675-4742`) does not set `IsLocked`. ServerSync `:702-727` returns unlocked when neither force lock nor locking config exists; `:1057-1063` permits changes while unlocked. The bundled **separate** SkillManager sync sets `IsLocked=true` (`:3295-3302`), which does not lock the main smith gear settings. Existing Lembitu.Callings hooks address profession death loss, not config locking (`src/plugins/Lembitu.Callings/SkillHooks.cs:39-48`).

**Proposal:** do not add a nonexistent config key. Keep this desirable profession only if upstream provides a release that sets main `configSync.IsLocked = true` or registers an actual locking entry (then pin that entry on). Otherwise a reviewed, explicit compatibility fix must lock this main sync before admission; absent that, reject BlacksmithingExpanded under the strict Pack rule. This is a prerequisite to trustworthy balancing, not an argument to replace its profession. No runtime exploit was attempted (forbidden by brief); confidence high static evidence, client-edit rejection is the future acceptance surface.

## 3. Boss modifiers: readability and composition variance

Ordinary groups roll at ~2.64% each (8×0.33 or 6×0.44); probability any modifier is ~10.15%. Boss groups fire ~25% each; expected count is ~1, but independent groups with `Max4` allow none or several. Four-group boss no-affix chance is about 31.6%; two-or-more chance about 26%. Enforcers average two with their separate table. Evidence `levels.yml:59-95,104-140`, `karma.yml:31-49`, `sighsorry.CreatureManager.cfg:50-62`.

These are not just damage riders: armored 30% reduction is ×1.429 effective HP; adaptive 50% reduction against remembered type can be ×2 for a specialized damage lane; vortex 50% projectile ignore can be roughly ×2 ranged attempts; chameleon rotates immunity. AttackSpeed +30% changes attack cadence independently of hit damage. Vampiric heals for 30% actual direct damage; reaping can grow max HP up to its listed cap even though deathward/regenerating were disabled. `levels.yml:70,74-77,89-91,115,119-122,134-136`.

**Proposal:** `sighsorry.CreatureManager.cfg [2 - Levels] Boss Modifiers = Max1`. Additionally set `Boss.modifiers.chameleon: 0, 10`, `Boss.modifiers.vortex: 0, 0.5`, `Boss.modifiers.adaptive: 0, 0.5` in levels.yml for first launch if a deterministic group-composition target matters more than novelty. Keep other chances unchanged: this deliberately lowers expected boss affix count rather than silently redistributing weights. Leave ordinary and Enforcer caps/chances as currently enforced. Confidence high mechanics / medium preference; Shakedown decides whether one readable affix suffices.

## 4. Offensive stacking, resistance floor, and elixirs

EpicLoot 0.14.13 patches `ItemData.GetDamage(int,float)` (`EpicLoot.cs:33937-33952`); adds elemental/physical riders relative to weapon total, multiplies physical/elemental separately, then applies its additive `modifyAll` bucket (`:34019-34060`). Oathbound then independently multiplies hits through `SEMan.ModifyAttack` and target-side `Character.RPC_Damage`: melee and critical (`Oathbound.cs:671-674`), Ranger distance/bleed/falling/deadeye (`:3128-3140`), Mage burn/chill/charged/near/shatter (`:2105-2122`). Herbalist Berserker explicitly invokes base ModifyAttack then multiplies the hit (`Herbalist.cs:437-441`) by configured 1.25 (`blacks7ar.Herbalist.cfg:34-36`). These are sequential factors, not one combined additive pool.

For illustration only [INFERENCE], a weapon with a 20% EpicLoot damage roll, +10% smith physical damage, 20% class multiplier and Berserker gives `.85*1.10*1.20*1.20*1.25 = 1.683` baseline damage before criticals/skill/target debuffs. It is not valid to add 10+20+20+25 and call it +75%; nor to apply smith to every magical elemental rider without checking damage type. The current smith overlay disables its elemental riders and per-upgrade percentage bonus (`org.bepinex.plugins.blacksmithingexpanded.cfg:25-47`), keeping this layer restrained. Talents are code-defined, not exposed per-node knobs; their effects also include sustain, stamina economy, active burst and defense, so a single global player multiplier would misrepresent class power.

ProgressivePowers allows one passive attunement; it is not seven simultaneously stacked powers (`MidnightsFX.ProgressivePowers.cfg:13-17`). Its stock Bonemass adds physical resistance entries, poison resistance and flat armor (`Powers.cs:2000-2019`); Eikthyr stamina/dodge and other powers change sustainable output or defense. Server config binders are admin-only (`Powers.cs:323-395`). **Keep `Max Attuned Powers = 1`, `Block Power Activation = true`.**

AdditiveDamageModifier's actual scope is resistance combination: `Additive.cs:1583-1608` sums resistance/weakness deltas. `:1658-1670` floors the resulting per-type resistance multiplier; `:1864-1869` applies configured floors only in player context. It does **not** cap outgoing talents, lifesteal, class shields, armor reduction, dodge/ignore procs or immunity implemented by separate hit cancellation. The enum `Ignore` bypasses even this floor (`:1663-1665`), so the overlay comment's “always takes at least a quarter” is too broad. It is a floor at the resistance stage, not a floor on final post-armor damage.

**Proposal:** retain all seven current `Player Minimum Damage Taken Percent - <type> = 25` keys and Berserker `Damage Multiplier = 1.25`; do not lower these merely to compensate enemy tails. Explain floor scope accurately to the operator. Defender elixir is a tank stance, not free damage: its code multiplies outgoing attacks ×0.2 (`Herbalist.cs:678-682`) and provides immunity-style resistance entries (`:735-751`); the configured floor tempers normal immune entries but not unrelated Ignore/cancel paths. Keep `DefendersElixir Recipe = true`, with boss-night documentation of its DPS trade-off. Confidence high; measure class-specific sustained/burst DPS and defensive effective health before changing EpicLoot effect tables.

## 5. Added creatures: scaling coverage versus rewards

### SeaAnimals 0.3.9

The mod's embedded library named `CreatureManager` is a prefab/spawn registration helper, **not** sighsorry's difficulty mod. Natural SeaAnimals creatures instantiate Characters and use vanilla SpawnSystem; sighsorry globally processes nonplayer Character/Humanoid Start (`CreatureManager.cs:9016-9025`) and unknown/managed sources default to full level/stat/affix rolls (`:19058-19074`). Its biome is resolved at position (`:17548`), so ocean creatures get Ocean Hard weights and coastal creatures their actual shore biome; no prefab whitelist restricts scaling to vanilla enemies.

Primary roster examples: SeaAnimals decompile `/tmp/lembitu-grill/ModChecks/decompiles/SeaAnimals.dll.cs:478,507,534,562,587,611,630,649,668,688,710,732` places Orca/Dolphin/WhiteShark/RightWhale in Ocean/DeepNorth, Crocodile in BlackForest/Swamp, BlueShark in Meadows, Hammerhead in Plains, TigerShark in Mistlands, blue turtle in BlackForest. Base asset HP: `/tmp/lembitu-grill/ModChecks/bundles/creature-health.json:3-96`: BlueShark 30, GreenTurtle 40, BlueTurtle 80, RedTurtle 160, Crocodile 200, squid 250, hammerhead 300, tiger/leatherback 350, dolphin/white/whale shark 400, whale/orca 500. Thus some ocean encounters are substantially tougher than low-HP vanilla mobs even at identical biome multipliers.

Oathbound death eligibility uses hostile relationships, excludes tames and blood summons, and otherwise accepts mod Characters (`Oathbound.cs:12542-12551`). XP is based on max HP, `clamp(floor(2*sqrt(HP)),10,80)` then ×4 for boss (`Core.cs:1987-1996`); max HP is included in death report (`Oathbound.cs:11790`). Thus 500 base HP ×3.48 average ocean scaling is already at the 80 nonboss cap. **This is not proof of an easy XP farm:** hostility, attacks, pursuit and encounter frequency determine effort, and tamed pet slaughter is excluded. Low-risk hostile shoreline animals are a plausible farm [INFERENCE], requiring observation.

Normal meat drops come from SeaAnimals' own CharacterDrop definitions (e.g. `SeaAnimals.dll.cs:629,648,667`). **EpicLoot is not automatic per-biome loot:** `EpicLoot.cs:18568-18578` does exact name lookup, and prior default `loottables.json` has no SA_ entries. A mod creature can receive CM scaling/XP and still produce no EpicLoot gear/shards. Vanilla star-quantity flags also differ per registered drop; parity is not guaranteed merely by stars.

**Proposal:** add explicit EpicLoot named aliases, not broad wildcard drops: `SA_BlueShark -> Tier0Mob`, `SA_Crocodile -> Tier3Mob`, `SA_HammerHeadShark -> Tier5Mob`, `SA_TigerShark -> Tier7Mob`, `SA_HumboldtSquid` and hostile ocean sharks -> `Tier5Mob`, each with JSON `{ "Object": "<prefab>", "RefObject": "<tier>" }` under LootTables in a server-synced EpicLoot override. Do not give peaceful turtles/dolphins/whales magic loot by default; confirm their hostility first. These exact refs exist in default table `loottables.json:1686-1835`. Confidence high lookup/scaling, medium proposed reward tiers. Shakedown: observe prefab ID, HP/level, hostile XP, meat count, and magic roll, including after zone reload.

## 7. Companions and tames versus eight-player bosses

Hunter wolf formulas: health `(80 + 4*(level-1))*(1+petHealthBonus/100)` and damage `(10 + (level-1))*(1+petDamageBonus/100)` (`Core.cs:1816-1823`). Dragonsworn: `(100+3*(level-1))` health, `(12+(level-1))` damage, likewise talent-scaled (`:1328-1335`). At level 80 before pet talents, these are wolf 396 HP/89 hit and whelp 337 HP/91 hit. These are **per-hit, not DPS**. They also carry owner buffs/heals/marks; code caps published damage to tree maximum and normalizes actual pet hits to that value (`Oathbound.cs:1262,1756`). Pet HP is explicitly set at spawn and continuously refreshed (`:9396-9399,10268-10271,17294-17296,17874-17876`). Wild-star HP ×2N should not be blindly applied to them.

CM recognizes vanilla player SpawnAbility sources (`CreatureManager.cs:8665,8677`) as player summons with baseline-only stats/no rolls; Oathbound companions are directly instantiated and tagged afterward, so automatic recognition is not established. Runtime pet overrides mean this is a cross-owner interaction, not proof that CM grants every pet wild stars. Generic captured wild tames retain assigned stats/modifier state; breeding has its own baseline-only policy with modifier rolling (`:19064-19071`). High-star Lox/Asksvin/wolves therefore may contribute more power than class pets, especially in repeat boss farms. Hard's enemy-damage branch triggers on nonplayer attackers (`vanilla Character.cs:2268-2272`), so tame/companion output may also receive hard damage scaling; at nonplayer targets the ×0.85 branch also applies (`:2414-2418`). Exact effect across companion attack customizations requires runtime ordering evidence.

**Proposal:** retain pets as a core class identity; no invented companion scaling config. Keep `SharedExperience = false` and current party XP integration, which credits companion kills to owners rather than making pets another party member. Shakedown boss roster must log pets, summons and brought tames. If tame armies undermine the cooperative target, specify a server-owned tame-to-boss damage rule as a separate design decision; do not nerf all vanilla wolves or all Hunters through `Global.damage` (that also nerfs enemies). Confidence high independent formulas; insufficient data for a numerical pet nerf.
