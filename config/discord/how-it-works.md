# How Lembitu works
Lembitu is a modded Valheim server for a small group: **MMORPG-lite Valheim for rival guilds that need each other.**

- **Harder than vanilla, no PvP at all.** Bosses fight like a five-player raid; ordinary creatures have twice the health and sometimes roll modifiers.
- **Guilds are rivals and partners at once.** Each guild holds its own start region, territory and vault, and trades with the others through the Market.
- **Progress is personal.** Boss unlocks belong to the players who were there, not to the world or the guild.
- **Seven ways to grow:** class level, a talent tree, professions, gear tiers, boss powers, guild levels and magic.
- **Pace:** an active group should reach the last boss in about a month. Playing alone is possible but slow; time alone pays best in building, gathering, crafting and the guild.

Read the posts below once; the in-game guide (F1) has the same rules in more detail.
---
## Joining
1. Get the **Player** role from the owner; the password is in #password.
2. Install the current client Pack from the latest post in #announcements. The server admits only the exact Pack, so update when a new one is announced.
3. Press **Join Lembitu** on the main menu. You type the password once.

- **One character per Steam account.** The server keeps your character and saves it every five minutes, so a crash costs a few minutes at most.
- Up to **10 players** at once. Keyboard and mouse only.
- **Daily restart at 06:00 Oslo time**, with warnings 10, 5 and 1 minute ahead in game and in #status.
- **Live web map:** https://lembitu-map.astral.ee shows the world, everyone's position and their guild.
- **Voice:** proximity voice chat is built into the Pack; nearby players hear you, quieter with distance.

## Your first evening
Everyone wakes at the **sacrificial stones**, where the **Oathstone** stands. The first evening is shared there.

1. **Take an oath** at the Oathstone to choose your class. No oath, no class XP.
2. **Choose a Calling** (Calling button in the skills window, confirmed at the Oathstone): four focus professions.
3. **Found or join a guild** with F9: FOUND A GUILD (free), INVITATIONS, or GUILD LIST to apply.
4. **Claim a start region.** A guild leader whose guild has no region uses one of the **portal stones** in the ring around the sacrificial stones. A window names the region and shows where it lies; confirm to claim it. One region per guild, first come, first served.

From then on the region is your guild's home: members respawn there and its portal stone takes them there. Leave the guild and you go back to the stones. A bed you place always overrides your respawn.

## Guilds: membership
- **At most five members, at most three guilds** (one per start region). Founding is free.
- **Ranks and permissions:** up to eight ranks. The leader decides who may invite, use the vault, build, use wards and conduct diplomacy.
- **Guild chat:** start a chat line with `/g`. The guild name shows above members' heads and beside them on the web map.
- **Vault:** a shared store, 40 stacks to start, opened from the guild window or the guild board. Coins are kept separately.
- **Wards:** bind your ward to the guild with Shift+E on it; members with ward access are then permitted on it.
- **Boss keys are never shared** with absent members. To unlock a biome you have to be at the fight.
- **Diplomacy tab:** propose alliances, declare war or make peace with the other guilds. There is still no PvP. Relations decide who may cross your territory border.
- Leaving or being kicked has no waiting period before you can join another guild.

## Guilds: banner, territory and buildings
**The banner.** A guild banner, built from the hammer's Guild tab, claims a **square territory** around it: 50 m on a side, plus 10 m per territory upgrade. Territories of different guilds stay at least 20 m apart. Inside, only members with ward access may build, remove pieces, open doors and use containers.

**The barrier.** Outsiders cannot walk in. Players from other guilds and players without a guild are stopped at the border; a glowing curtain in the guild colour shows where it is. Members always pass, and so do **allied guilds** and guilds **at war** with the owner. Entering a territory shows its name on screen.

**Levelling. Choose the banner spot carefully.** When the banner is placed, and again whenever the territory grows, the ground is flattened to the banner's height with a gentle slope at the edges. Trees, rocks and bushes in the way are removed for good. Ground under existing buildings is left alone.

**Guild buildings** go up inside the territory, built by members with the buildings permission. Each one costs the materials of its pieces, and more are allowed as the guild levels. Examples:
- **Armory** (+40 gear stacks in the vault) and **warehouse** (+60 material stacks).
- **Longhouse**: +2 comfort within 15 m.
- **Barracks**: members may respawn there.
- **Stone tower or watchtower**: warns guild chat when an outsider enters.
- Also a smithy, kitchen, tavern, workshop, bathhouse, trophy hall, training ground, market stall, guardhouse and gate.

A **guild board** at each building's entrance holds the guild functions and removes the whole building.

## Guilds: levels and upgrades
Guilds level from **1 to 30**. Guild XP comes from:
- **Members' activity:** kills, crafting, felling trees, mining, crops, fish, building, taming and travel.
- **Bosses:** 1,500 XP the first time the guild kills each boss, and 150 for every boss kill after that.
- **Achievements:** 300 for bronze, 1,000 for silver, 3,000 for gold.
- **Coin donations:** every 10 coins buys 1 guild XP. The coins leave the economy, so trade feeds the guild.

Each level needs 15% more XP than the last and gives **one guild point**. Spend points on **banner upgrades** by pressing E on the banner:
- **Territory:** +10 m on a side.
- **Vault:** +10 stacks.
- **Monster ward:** from guild level 10; creatures stop spawning inside the territory.
- **Comfort:** at guild levels 12 and 20, paid in materials and coins.

The member cap stays at five; there is no upgrade that raises it.

## Parties, the Market and cooperation
**Parties** are who you hunt with: invite only, up to eight people, and they may mix guilds. Press P. A party shares exactly two things:
- **Map positions.**
- **Kill XP:** a kill's class XP is split among the killer's party members within 100 m of the creature. A full party of four earns 150% of the kill between them.

**The Market (F7)** is server-wide and works while the other side is offline: coin sales, buy orders, jobs and a bank. Every payout carries a 5% fee.

**Why cooperate?** No guild can do everything well. Specialists make better goods: a smith's gear hits harder, a cook's dishes give more and keep longer, a brewer's meads last longer. Their quality survives the Market. Bosses are tuned for five players, so smaller guilds and late joiners do better in mixed parties. A late joiner catches up by being carried through a boss again; nobody skips a boss.

## Progression
- **Boss keys (personal).** The world writes no keys. When a boss dies, everyone within 100 m gets its key, and nobody else. A key unlocks crafting, wearing, cooking and eating that biome's gear and food, plus boss powers and summons. Building, repairs, taming, boats and portals are never locked.
- **Class (Oathbound).** Thirteen classes, chosen at the Oathstone. Kills give class XP and every level gives a talent point, up to level 80. Each class keeps its own progress, and respec is free at the Oathstone. A class's power counts at most level 10, plus 10 for every boss key you hold, so grinding kills never outruns the bosses.
- **Calling and professions.** Your four focus professions (two Land, one Craft, one Road) level at full speed and keep their XP when you die. Every other profession slows down past level 10. Professions give perks, never crafting blocks: anyone may craft what they know.
- **Gear (Epic Loot).** Magic drops, enchanting and rarities on top of each biome's gear.
- **Boss powers** are passive and grow with every boss kill you attend.
---
## Danger, death and the world
- **Death costs something.** You lose half your progress toward the next class level (never a level). Each non-focus skill loses 5%, but never below your floor, which rises by 10 per boss key. Your tombstone holds what vanilla takes, so go back for it.
- **Food spoils.** Most dishes keep 2–4 days of server time. Fresh food gives 10% more and stale food less. Jerky, sausages, smoked fish, smoked moose meat and honey never spoil. Nothing spoils in the Mountains or the Deep North, and an Icebox stops the clock at home.
- **Raids** come less often than in vanilla, as Oathbound sieges. Lit fires keep spawns out of a base, and the guild monster ward protects a whole territory.
- **Portals:** press E on a wood portal to name it and pick its destination from a list of every portal. Ore and metal never pass through.
- **The world** is about 1.76× vanilla's area, with points of interest at the usual density.
